SKIRMISH

An open browser RTS, built from scratch. Grow the little village: gather, build, train.

How to play

  • Click a villager to select it; drag to select a group; Shift+click adds or removes one; double-click selects every villager on screen. Click empty ground to deselect.
  • Right-click a tree, bush, gold or stone to gather it; right-click the ground to move. Orders go only to the selected villagers.
  • Drag right button / WASD / arrows to pan, mouse wheel to zoom. Click the minimap to jump the camera.
  • Commands panel (bottom-left): with a villager selected, build a House (25 wood, +5 population room) or a Farm (60 wood, 175 food). Select the Town Center to train villagers (50 food) — training stalls at the population cap.
  • Esc pauses.
