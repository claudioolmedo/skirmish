Skirmish II — the generated sound set

Every sound below was generated by our own pipeline (tools/sound-gen) — DSP synthesis for effects, local Kokoro TTS for the voices, original phrases only. Nothing is sampled or copied from any other game. In the game these play contextually: the class voice answers your clicks, work strikes match the resource, the town gets bells and soldiers get the horn.
