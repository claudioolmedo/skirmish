Play the test build

This is the live M3 test build — a working economy, construction, and now combat. Send villagers to gather wood, berries, gold and stone, train more at the Town Center, then build: Houses raise your population room, Farms grow food your villagers harvest. Your Scout Cavalry rides with the village from the start; sheep, boars and deer roam the map, and a Barracks trains Militia and Archers to hunt and defend. The villager is already our own original art; the landscape still uses clearly-marked placeholder test art while our own sprites replace it, piece by piece. The game opens fullscreen, on its own page.

Play the test build →

How to play: click a villager to select it, then right-click a tree, bush, gold or stone to gather — or the ground to move. Train villagers at the Town Center, but training stalls at the population cap: build Houses (+5 room, 25 wood), Farms (175 food, 60 wood) and a Barracks (175 wood) from the build menu. Right-click an animal to hunt it; build the Barracks to train Militia and Archers. Pan with WASD / arrow keys or right-mouse drag; zoom with the mouse wheel; Esc pauses.

Why Skirmish

Big strategy games want new hardware, a huge download and a store account. Skirmish is the opposite: a complete little RTS that lives in a browser tab — for anyone, on any machine.

Same game, every machine

The simulation is pure Rust with fixed-point math: same seed, same commands, same result — on every computer. That is what makes fair multiplayer and replays possible.

Runs on anything

One build for every computer. Only the visuals scale to your machine — the simulation never does — so an old laptop and a gaming rig play on even terms.

Original & clean-room

Every sprite and every line of code is made for Skirmish, from scratch. Inspired by the classics — never copied from them.

Built in the open

The source is on GitHub and the game grows in public, milestone by milestone. Play it, read the code, follow along — or fork it.

Roadmap
