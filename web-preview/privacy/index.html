Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24

The short version

  • Skirmish II needs no accounts and no sign-up to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal data.
  • Single-player games run entirely on your own device.
  • Online play passes game commands through a relay between the two players in a room. The relay keeps anonymous totals only.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and understand usage in aggregate. Google may set cookies for that measurement — see Website analytics below.
  • Today the ad slots show the project's own placeholders. When advertising through Google AdSense is activated, Google may use cookies to serve ads — see Advertising below.

Who operates this site

Skirmish II is an open-source browser game, operated by Claudio Olmedo ("Skirmish II", "we", "us"). The site is skirmish.online, and the source code is public at github.com/claudioolmedo/skirmish.

What we collect

Nothing personal. There are no accounts and no registration forms. We do not ask for, receive, or store names, email addresses, or other information that identifies you. The one measurement tool on the site is Google Analytics — see Website analytics below.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and understand, in aggregate, how the site and the game are used — for example, how many people visit, which pages they open, and roughly how long they stay. Google Analytics is run by Google, and Google may set cookies in your browser for that measurement. The game itself still creates no account and no personal profile for you, and we do not use Analytics to identify individual players. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; the game works the same either way. Google's own description of how it uses information from sites that use its services is at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Playing single-player

A single-player match runs completely inside your browser, on your device. No game data leaves your device when you play alone.

Playing online

Online matches are one-versus-one between two players who share a room link. Because both players' browsers must stay in perfect sync, your game commands (unit orders, builds, and similar in-game actions) travel through a relay service hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them between the two players in the room. The relay does not run the game and does not keep match records.

So the game can stay healthy and improve, the relay counts matches in aggregate only. It stores totals like these:

  • how many matches were played,
  • their total duration, measured in game ticks,
  • how many matches fell out of sync ("desyncs"),
  • totals per server region (for example, North America West).

The relay stores no IP addresses, no per-match records, and no player identifiers. These counters cannot be traced back to any individual player. Match replays, when offered, are recorded by your own browser and saved only if you choose to download them.

Feedback you choose to send

At the end of a match, the game offers an optional feedback box: a star rating (one to five) and a short text field answering "What broke for you?". If you press Send, that rating and the text you typed are sent to our relay and stored there so we can read what players are running into and publish aggregate results (for example, the average star rating). Nothing else travels with it — no name, no account, no identifier of any kind; the relay records only the rating, your text, the match result and its length in game ticks, and the game version. If you never press Send, nothing is sent. Feedback is entirely optional and the game plays exactly the same either way.

There is also a report / contribution form — in the game's pause menu and at skirmish.online/feedback — with a kind (urgent problem, contribution idea, or other), a message, and an optional name field. If you send it, the kind, your message, and the name if you wrote one are stored together with the feedback, so urgent problems can be read first. The name is whatever you type; it is not verified and is not tied to any account.

Storage in your browser

The site saves small preferences in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • your graphics quality choice,
  • your player name, if you set one in the lobby,
  • whether you dismissed the advertising notice, and
  • whether you already sent end-of-match feedback (so the game does not keep asking).

Apart from the cookies Google Analytics may set (see Website analytics above), this site sets no tracking cookies of its own. You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings; the site will simply ask again.

Advertising

The ad slots on this site are always labeled "Advertisement". Today they show the project's own house placeholders — friendly messages from Skirmish II itself, not third-party ads.

When Google AdSense is activated on this site, ads will be served by Google. Google and its partners may then use cookies and similar technologies to serve, measure, and — depending on your choices — personalize ads, as described in Google's own documentation: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

You stay in control of that:

  • Manage ad personalization in your Google account at adssettings.google.com.
  • Block or delete third-party cookies at any time in your browser settings. The game works the same either way.

Whatever the ad settings, ads on Skirmish II never cover the game and never interrupt play.

Children

Skirmish II is a general-audience game. As explained above, we collect no personal data from anyone — including children.
