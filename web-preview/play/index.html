How to play

  • Click a villager to select it; drag to select a group; Shift+click adds or removes one; double-click selects every villager on screen. Click empty ground to deselect. One selection holds at most 60 units.
  • Right-click a tree, bush, gold or stone to gather it; right-click the ground to move. Orders go only to the selected villagers. Shift+right-click queues the order — the unit finishes what it is doing, then runs the queued ones in order. Right-clicking the minimap orders there too.
  • Control groups: Ctrl+1–9 stores the selection, the number recalls it, pressing it twice centers the camera, Shift+number adds the group to the selection. . selects the next idle villager, , the next idle soldier (watch the counters in the top bar), H jumps to the Town Center.
  • Drag right button / WASD / arrows to pan, mouse wheel to zoom. Click the minimap to jump the camera.
  • Commands panel (bottom-left): with a villager selected, build a House (25 wood, +5 population room), the three camps (100 wood each — villagers drop their loads at the nearest building that takes them: Lumber Camp for wood, Mill for food, Mining Camp for gold and stone), a Farm (60 wood, 175 food — needs a Mill first) or a Barracks (175 wood). Select the Town Center to train villagers (50 food) — training stalls at the population cap.
  • Force drop-off: with loaded villagers selected, right-click your Town Center or a camp — they haul what they carry in right away, then walk back to their work on their own.
  • Ages & research: at the Town Center, research Loom (50 gold — villagers +15 HP and armor) and advance to the Feudal Age (500 food; needs any 2 Dark-Age buildings besides Houses and Farms). Research shares the Town Center queue with villager training. Feudal unlocks the Archery Range (Archers move there from the Barracks), the Stable (Scout Cavalry) and the Blacksmith (Fletching, Scale Mail, Padded Archer Armor) — and the Barracks gains the Man-at-Arms upgrade. Your age shows in the top bar.
  • Rally points: with the Town Center, Barracks, Archery Range or Stable selected, right-click the map to set where new units walk to. Right-click a resource and new villagers go straight to work on it.
  • Combat: you start with a Scout Cavalry and sheep nearby. Walk any unit next to a sheep to claim it; right-click an animal with a unit selected to attack and hunt it — the carcass feeds your villagers (a hunter carries 35). Boars fight back and chase whoever hurt them; lure one toward your village instead of fighting it alone.
  • Select the Barracks to train Militia (60 food, 20 gold); Archers (25 wood, 45 gold) train at the Archery Range once you reach the Feudal Age. Idle Militia and Archers guard against boars on their own; damaged units show an HP bar.
  • The match: Sandbox builds in peace. Against the AI, an enemy village mirrors yours across the map — it gathers, builds and attacks in waves, wearing red (you wear blue). Win by Conquest: destroy its Town Center, every building and every unit. Lose all of yours and the match is lost. Click the enemy Town Center to check its health.
  • Esc pauses.

Esc closes this guide too.
